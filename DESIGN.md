## Visual Direction

`min` uses a dense, minimal, dark desktop-product surface inspired by Linear: near-black canvas, charcoal panels, fine hairline borders, restrained typography, and a single lavender-blue accent for primary action, focus, and identity state.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#010102`
- Surface 1: `#0f1011`
- Surface 2: `#141516`
- Surface 3: `#191a1b`
- Hairline: `#23252a`
- Strong hairline: `#34343a`
- Ink: `#f7f8f8`
- Muted ink: `#8a8f98`
- Accent: `#5e6ad2`
- Accent hover: `#828fff`

## UI Rules

- Main screen is the app itself, not a marketing page.
- Use compact 8px-radius panels and controls.
- Keep security state visible but quiet.
- Prefer functional icons in controls.
- Avoid decorative color fields; accent appears only where it communicates action, identity, or focus.
- Message surfaces must remain readable, scan-friendly, and stable at narrow widths.
